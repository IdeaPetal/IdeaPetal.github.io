1. Introduction

Welcome to FindAlphabet ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application FindAlphabet (the "App"). Please read this privacy policy carefully. 2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information We Do NOT Collect

FindAlphabet is designed with children's privacy in mind. We do NOT collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email address, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Photos or videos
  • Contact lists
  • Biometric data
  • Any personally identifiable information from children

2.2 Information We May Collect

The App may collect the following non-personal information:

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, and device identifiers (for app functionality only)
  • Game Progress Data: Locally stored game progress, settings, and preferences (stored on your device only)
  • Technical Data: Crash reports and performance analytics (anonymized, no personal identification)

2.3 Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services that have their own privacy policies:

  • Analytics Services: We may use COPPA-compliant analytics services to understand app usage patterns. These services do not collect personal information from children.
  • Advertising Services: If the App displays advertisements, we use COPPA-compliant ad networks that do not use behavioral advertising or track children.
  • In-App Purchases: Purchases are processed through platform stores (Google Play, Apple App Store) which have their own privacy policies.

3. How We Use Information

We use the limited information we collect solely for:

  • Providing and maintaining the App's functionality
  • Improving the App's performance and user experience
  • Fixing bugs and technical issues
  • Understanding general usage patterns (in aggregate, anonymized form)

We do NOT use information for:

  • Marketing or advertising to children
  • Creating user profiles
  • Sharing with third parties for marketing purposes
  • Behavioral tracking or targeting

4. Data Storage and Security

All game data is stored locally on your device using encrypted storage. We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

FindAlphabet is designed for children and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Our practices include:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13
  • We do not use behavioral advertising targeted at children
  • We use COPPA-compliant third-party services only
  • All in-app purchases require a parent gate (math verification)
  • We do not share children's information with third parties for marketing purposes

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can delete such information.

Parents have the right to:
